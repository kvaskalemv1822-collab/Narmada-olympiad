Narmada Valley Maths & Science Olympiad (NVMSO)

Official Terms & Conditions — NVMSO 2026

1. Eligibility, Registration & Exam Modes

  1. Student Eligibility: Only eligible school students within the declared classes may register with accurate personal, class, and school details.
  2. School Routing & Authorization: Participation is coordinated through registered schools and authorized school organizers. School coordinators must obtain proper Principal/School Management approval before enrolling students.
  3. Exam Modes & Fees: Applicable fees apply based on the selected mode (for example, Online Mode and Offline Desk / OMR Mode). Registration approval is subject to fee verification.
  4. Duplicate Prevention: Duplicate Roll Numbers or duplicate UTR entries are flagged or blocked to maintain fair records.

2. Payment, UTR Verification & Coordinator Settlement

  1. Valid UTR Submission: For UPI/QR or online payments, candidates or desks must submit a valid, unique UTR reference. Unverified, fake, or duplicate UTR entries may be rejected or held.
  2. Cash Collection & Settlement: Organizers collecting offline cash fees must maintain accurate records, remit net payable amounts to NVMSO as per settlement rules, and submit valid UTR proof.
  3. Commission & Payouts: Direct commission (15%) and referral commission (5%) apply strictly under NVMSO's declared calculation base and verified registration rules. Coordinators must provide a valid UPI ID for payouts; incorrect UPI details or suspected misuse may delay or hold payouts.

3. Account Security & Exam Discipline

  1. Account & PIN Security: Coordinators must keep their Organizer Code, password, and 6-digit Security PIN confidential. Any unauthorized sharing or misuse of credentials is the responsibility of the account holder.
  2. Fair Play & Proctoring: Online exams monitor tab switching and suspicious activity; repeated warnings or rule violations may lead to automatic submission or disqualification. Cheating, impersonation, or question paper leakage is strictly prohibited.

4. Results, Rankings, Medals & Data Privacy

  1. Results & Awards: Marks, percentages, class-wise rankings, certificates, and medals (such as Gold, Silver, and Bronze) are determined under NVMSO's official evaluation rules and answer keys, and are final subject to administrative review of errors.
  2. Student Data Privacy: Student information is collected with appropriate school/parental authorization solely for examination administration, results, and certificates, and is not sold or shared with unauthorized third parties.

5. Intellectual Property & Governing Law

  1. Intellectual Property: All NVMSO branding, logos, question papers, software/UI elements, and certificate templates belong to NVMSO or their respective rights holders and may not be copied or commercially reused without permission.
  2. Governing Law & Jurisdiction: These terms are governed by applicable laws in India, and any disputes shall be subject to the competent local courts/jurisdiction applicable to NVMSO operations.

6. Exam Cancellation & Refund Policy

  1. General Non-Refundable Rule: Registration fees are non-refundable once processed under normal examination conditions.
  2. Duplicate Transactions: In case of duplicate online transactions, the verified excess amount will be refunded within 5–7 working days.
  3. Official Exam Cancellation Rules:
    • Cash Payers: Cash refunds, where applicable, will be processed through the respective School Organizer / School Desk.
    • Online / UPI Payers: Eligible refunds will be credited back to the original UPI ID / Payment Account within 7–10 working days.
Note: Please review these terms with your legal or compliance advisor before final public rollout and update specific court jurisdiction or fee/payout policy details as needed.
