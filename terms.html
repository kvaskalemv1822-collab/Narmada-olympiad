Narmada Valley Maths & Science Olympiad (NVMSO)

Terms & Conditions

1. Participation in NVMSO is strictly routed through registered schools and school organizers.

2. Students must provide accurate information during registration. Duplicate Roll Numbers or UTRs are automatically blocked.

3. The exam platform monitors tab switching and suspicious activities; multiple warnings result in automatic submission.

Exam Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. Registration fees are non-refundable once processed under normal conditions.

2. **Duplicate Transactions:** In case of duplicate online transactions, the extra amount will be refunded within 5-7 working days.

3. **Official Exam Cancellation Rules:**

  • **Cash Payers:** Cash refund will be issued directly through their respective **School Organizer** / School Desk.
  • **Online / UPI Payers:** Direct refund will be credited back to the **original UPI ID / Payment Account** within 7-10 working days.
